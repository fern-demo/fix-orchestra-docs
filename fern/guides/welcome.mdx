## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a session">
    Ask your Acme account manager for a `SenderCompID`/`TargetCompID` pair and the certification environment host.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log on">
    Send a [Logon](/fix/messages/logon) message with `HeartBtInt` (108) set to 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an order">
    Send a [NewOrderSingle](/fix/messages/new-order-single) and wait for an [ExecutionReport](/fix/messages/execution-report).
  </Step>
</Steps>
