---
title: "PaymentStreamPaymentDates"
subtitle: "PaymentStreamPaymentDates is a subcomponent of the PaymentStream component used to specify the payment dates of the stream."
---

<Badge intent="note">Component</Badge> <Badge intent="note" minimal>Common</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

For equity return swaps this component is used to specify the interim price payment dates and the PaymentStreamFinalPricePaymentDate component is used to specify the final price payment date.
