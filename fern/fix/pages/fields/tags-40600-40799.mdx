## Tag 40634: UnderlyingPaymentStreamInitialRate

<llms-ignore>
<Badge intent="info" minimal>Tag 40634</Badge> <Badge intent="note" minimal>Percentage</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>
</llms-ignore>

<llms-only>
Tag 40634 · Percentage · Added FIX.5.0SP2
</llms-only>
