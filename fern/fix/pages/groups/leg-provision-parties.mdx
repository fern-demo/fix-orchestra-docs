---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40533</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

The fields LegProvisionPartyID(40534), LegProvisionPartyIDSource(40535) and LegProvisionPartyIDRole(40536) are conditionally required when any one these fields is specified.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegProvisionPartyIDs (40533)](/fix/fields/tags-40400-40599#tag-40533).
