<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40448</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

A swap may have one or more provisions.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegProvisions (40448)](/fix/fields/tags-40400-40599#tag-40448).
