---
title: "EntitlementGrp"
subtitle: "Conveys a list of entitlements for one specific party, or relationship between two parties. Each entitlement may be further limited or clarified using optional fields and components."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 1773</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoEntitlements (1773)](/fix/fields/tags-1600-1799#tag-1773).
