<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 41878</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

If the fixing days are not specified, then every day of the week will be a fixing day.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoUnderlyingPaymentScheduleFixingDays (41878)](/fix/fields/tags-41800-41999#tag-41878).
