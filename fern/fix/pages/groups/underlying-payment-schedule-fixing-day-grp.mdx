If the fixing days are not specified, then every day of the week will be a fixing day.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoUnderlyingPaymentScheduleFixingDays (41878)](/fix/fields/tags-41800-41999#tag-41878-nounderlyingpaymentschedulefixingdays).
