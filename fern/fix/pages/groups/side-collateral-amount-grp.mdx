---
title: "SideCollateralAmountGrp"
subtitle: "The SideCollateralAmountGrp component block is a repeating group that provides the current value of the collateral type on deposit for a side of the trade report. The currency of the collateral value may be optionally included."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 2691</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoSideCollateralAmounts (2691)](/fix/fields/tags-2600-2799#tag-2691).
