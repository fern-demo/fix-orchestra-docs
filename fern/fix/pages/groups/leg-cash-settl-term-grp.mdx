subtitle: "The LegCashSettlTermGrp is a repeating component within the InstrumentLeg component used to report cash settlement terms."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 41344</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Usage of LegCashSettlTermGrp must either include a known LegCashSettlAmount(41357) or provide the cash settlement term parameters needed to derive the cash settlement amount. LegCashSettlTermXID(41362) is provided for cross-referencing from an instance of the UnderlyingInstrument component through the UnderlyingSettlTermXIDRef(41315) field.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegCashSettlTerms (41344)](/fix/fields/tags-41200-41399#tag-41344).
