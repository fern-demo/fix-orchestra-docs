---
title: "UnderlyingDeliveryScheduleGrp"
subtitle: "The UnderlyingDeliveryScheduleGrp is a repeating subcomponent of the UnderlyingStream component used to detail step schedules associated with a delivery stream."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 41756</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Note: Holiday schedule is standard for the country and time zone and need not be specified.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoUnderlyingDeliverySchedules (41756)](/fix/fields/tags-41600-41799#tag-41756).
