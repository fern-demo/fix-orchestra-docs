When used within the PriceMovementGrp, the ClearingAccountTypeGrp specifies the type of account the price movement data is applicable for.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoClearingAccountTypes (1918)](/fix/fields/tags-1800-1999#tag-1918).
