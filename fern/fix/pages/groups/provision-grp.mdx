subtitle: "The ProvisionGrp is a repeating subcomponent of the Instrument component used to detail the additional terms and conditions associated with the instrument."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40090</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

A swap may have one or more provisions defined.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoProvisions (40090)](/fix/fields/tags-40000-40199#tag-40090).
