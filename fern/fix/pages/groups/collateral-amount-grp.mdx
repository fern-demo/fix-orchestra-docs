---
title: "CollateralAmountGrp"
subtitle: "The Collateral Amount Group component block is a repeating group that provides the current value of the collateral type on deposit. The currency of the collateral value may be optionally included."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 1703</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoCollateralAmounts (1703)](/fix/fields/tags-1600-1799#tag-1703).
