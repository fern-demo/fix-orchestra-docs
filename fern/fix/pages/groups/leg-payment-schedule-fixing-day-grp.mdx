Repeating group · NumInGroup 41530 · Added FIX.5.0SP2
</llms-only>

If the fixing days are not specified, then every day of the week will be a fixing day.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegPaymentScheduleFixingDays (41530)](/fix/fields/tags-41400-41599#tag-41530-nolegpaymentschedulefixingdays).
