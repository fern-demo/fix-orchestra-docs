<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40000</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoAdditionalTermBondRefs (40000)](/fix/fields/tags-40000-40199#tag-40000).
