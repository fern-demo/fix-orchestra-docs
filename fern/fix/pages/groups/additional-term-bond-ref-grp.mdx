Repeating group · NumInGroup 40000 · Added FIX.5.0SP2

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoAdditionalTermBondRefs (40000)](/fix/fields/tags-40000-40199#tag-40000-noadditionaltermbondrefs).
