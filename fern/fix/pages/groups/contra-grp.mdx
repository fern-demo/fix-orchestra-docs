<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 382</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.4.4</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoContraBrokers (382)](/fix/fields/tags-200-399#tag-382).
