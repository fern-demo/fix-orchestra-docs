<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 2345</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

When used in the PartyEntitlementRequest(35=CU) message it serves to provide filtering criteria for the results set.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoEntitlementTypes (2345)](/fix/fields/tags-2200-2399#tag-2345).

| 2402 | [EntitlementSubType](/fix/fields/tags-2400-2599#tag-2402) | EntitlementSubTypeCodeSet | Optional | Subtype of an entitlement specified in EntitlementType(1775). |
