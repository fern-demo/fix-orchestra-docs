---
title: "PaymentStubGrp"
subtitle: "The PaymentStubGrp is a repeating subcomponent of the StreamGrp component used to specify front and back stubs of the payment stream."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40872</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoPaymentStubs (40872)](/fix/fields/tags-40800-40999#tag-40872).
