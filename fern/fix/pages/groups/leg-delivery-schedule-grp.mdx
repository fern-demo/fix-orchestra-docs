Repeating group · NumInGroup 41408 · Added FIX.5.0SP2
</llms-only>

Note: Holiday schedule is standard for the country and time zone and need not be specified.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegDeliverySchedules (41408)](/fix/fields/tags-41400-41599#tag-41408-nolegdeliveryschedules).
