Repeating group · NumInGroup 40174 · Added FIX.5.0SP2
</llms-only>

The fields ProvisionPartyID(40175), ProvisionPartyIDSource(40176) and ProvisionPartyIDRole(40177) are conditionally required when any one these fields is specified.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoProvisionPartyIDs (40174)](/fix/fields/tags-40000-40199#tag-40174-noprovisionpartyids).
