Repeating group · NumInGroup 41037 · Added FIX.5.0SP2
</llms-only>

Note: Holiday schedule is standard for the country and time zone and need not be specified.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoDeliverySchedules (41037)](/fix/fields/tags-41000-41199#tag-41037-nodeliveryschedules).
