title: "UnderlyingPaymentStubGrp"
subtitle: "The UnderlyingPaymentStubGrp is a repeating subcomponent of the UnderlyingPaymentStream component used to specify front and back stubs in the payment stream."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40708</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoUnderlyingPaymentStubs (40708)](/fix/fields/tags-40600-40799#tag-40708).
