---
title: "DividendPeriodGrp"
subtitle: "DividendPeriodGrp is a repeating subcomponent within the DividendConditions component. It is used to specify the valuation and payments dates of the dividend leg of a dividend swap."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 42274</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoDividendPeriods (42274)](/fix/fields/tags-42200-42399#tag-42274).
