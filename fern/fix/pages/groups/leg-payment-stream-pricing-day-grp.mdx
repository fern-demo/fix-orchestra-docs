<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 41596</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

If the fixing days are not specified, then every day of the week will be a fixing day.

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegPaymentStreamPricingDays (41596)](/fix/fields/tags-41400-41599#tag-41596).

| 41598 | [LegPaymentStreamPricingDayNumber](/fix/fields/tags-41400-41599#tag-41598) | int | Optional | The occurrence of the day of week on which pricing takes place. |
