Repeating group · NumInGroup 2864 · Added FIX.5.0SP2

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoSideCollateralReinvestments (2864)](/fix/fields/tags-2800-2999#tag-2864-nosidecollateralreinvestments).
