Repeating group · NumInGroup 1812 · Added FIX.5.0SP2

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoDisclosureInstructions (1812)](/fix/fields/tags-1800-1999#tag-1812-nodisclosureinstructions).
