---
title: "LegDividendPeriodGrp"
subtitle: "LegDividendPeriodGrp is a repeating subcomponent within the LegDividendConditions component. It is used to specify the valuation and payments dates of the dividend leg of a dividend swap."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 42366</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegDividendPeriods (42366)](/fix/fields/tags-42200-42399#tag-42366).
