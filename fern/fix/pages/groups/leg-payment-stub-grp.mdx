---
title: "LegPaymentStubGrp"
subtitle: "The LegPaymentStubGrp is a repeating subcomponent of the LegPaymentStream component used to specify front and back stubs in the payment stream."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40418</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoLegPaymentStubs (40418)](/fix/fields/tags-40400-40599#tag-40418).
