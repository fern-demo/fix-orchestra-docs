<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 40191</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoProtectionTermEvents (40191)](/fix/fields/tags-40000-40199#tag-40191).
