---
title: "RootParties"
subtitle: "The RootParties component block is a version of the Parties component block used to provide root information regarding the owning and entering parties of a transaction."
---

<Badge intent="note">Repeating group</Badge> <Badge intent="info" minimal>NumInGroup 1116</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.4.4</Badge>

Each repetition starts with the first member listed. The count of repetitions is sent in [NoRootPartyIDs (1116)](/fix/fields/tags-1000-1199#tag-1116).
