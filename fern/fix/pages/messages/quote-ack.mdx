---

<Badge intent="info">MsgType CW</Badge> <Badge intent="note" minimal>Quotation Negotiation</Badge> <Badge intent="tip" minimal>Pre Trade</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

The QuoteAck(35=CW) is available for optional use to acknowledge the request to cancel an individual quote (QuoteCancel(35=Z) with QuoteCancelType(298) =5(Cancel specified sinqle quote)).
