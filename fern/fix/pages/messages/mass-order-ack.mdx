---
title: "MassOrderAck"
subtitle: "The mass order acknowledgement message is used to acknowledge the receipt of and the status for a MassOrder(35=DJ) message."
---

<Badge intent="info">MsgType DK</Badge> <Badge intent="note" minimal>Order Mass Handling</Badge> <Badge intent="tip" minimal>Trade</Badge> <Badge intent="success" minimal>Added FIX.5.0SP2</Badge>

The content of the acknowledgement depends on the setting of the field OrderResponseLevel(2427) in the MassOrder(35=DJ) message. Only the order status is provided and not the immediate executions which would lead to ExecutionReport messages.
